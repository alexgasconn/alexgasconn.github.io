01. About Me

I studied Telecommunications Engineering, and my profession is software engineering. At Indra, I develop and maintain software for telecommunications systems; beyond that domain, my strongest interests are Data Science and sports analytics.

I build data pipelines, interactive analysis tools and geospatial products that make GPS and training data easier to understand. My work spans performance metrics, prediction, route analysis and the software engineering needed to make those tools reliable and useful.

Alongside my engineering work, I completed postgraduate studies in Sports Analytics at UPC and Barça Innovation Hub. I bring the perspective of an active runner, cyclist and triathlete to the tools I build.

Sports data & performance

08 — 10

Most ambitious project · Endurance analytics

StravaStats

A free, feature-rich analytics platform built to bring premium-style training insights to every athlete. Connect a Strava account and explore 13 focused areas: training load and consistency, in-depth running, cycling and swimming analysis, athlete profile, race predictions, gear lifespan, searchable activities, calendar, historical weather, route maps, annual reports and an optional AI coach.

The Predictor blends Riegel scaling, VDOT-style performance transfer and personal curve fitting. Dedicated activity pages go deeper into streams, splits, climbs and sport-specific metrics, with GPX, CSV and JSON exports. Most analysis runs in the browser, backed by Strava OAuth and a lightweight Vercel API layer.

  • JavaScript ES modules
  • Strava API
  • Vercel Functions
  • Chart.js / D3
  • Leaflet
  • PWA
